# DESIGN.md — Collection (Godot rebuild) spec

The single source of truth for what gets built. Code follows this file; if they disagree, this file wins until Pete changes it.

**Origin:** the Collection mode from imok 1v1 (browser/three.js), rebuilt as its own Godot game. Numbers come from the last working code: `PostWorkCulture/1v1`, branch `collection-v1`, commit `76738cc`, `index.html`. Items marked **(Pete)** are Pete's design decisions and must not change without him. Everything else is how it was built and can be improved.

Units: metres, seconds. Y is up.

## 1. Pitch
8 players in a big two-floor mansion. Everyone spawns outside on the lawns with only a bat **(Pete, 26 Nov)**. Gold stars are hidden around the inside of the house, and guns (20 bullets, no reload) are lying about. Find 10 stars, then get back outside to the open shop (one of four in the lawn corners; it keeps moving) and stand at it to cash in. The first player to cash in wins. Getting knocked down costs you 2 stars, and dying loses all of them. Anyone holding 8 or more stars glows through walls and shows on everyone's minimap, so the leader is hunted.

## 3. Stars
- 6 on the map at most (`ON_MAP = 6`) **(Pete, 24 Nov: fewer stars out, spread out a lot more; was 14)**.
- Spawn inside the house (both floors) or, for 15% of them, on the roof; never outside **(Pete, 26 Nov: collectables mostly inside or on the roof)**.
- Spawn spot: walkable floor spot in the main connected nav area that a body fits in (no deep water), at least 10 m from every other star, visible pickup and living player **(Pete, 26 Nov: was 7)** (vertical separation over 3 m also counts as clear). Of 40 such spots the one furthest from the other stars is used (another floor counts as 12 m), so the 6 spread across the house.
- Pick-up: within 1.2 m horizontally and 1.6 m vertically. Not while downed, dead, doing a finisher, or already holding 10.
- Replacement: a taken star comes back 8–14 s later, at least 25 m from where it was taken (was: at once, 12 m away, plus one every 4 s while fewer were out).
- Look: chunky gold 5-point star, extruded ~0.07 m with bevelled edges, ~0.85 m across. Metal: colour `#ffc83a`, metalness 0.85, roughness 0.22, emissive `#a8700a` at 0.55. Floats 1.05 m up, spins ~1.6 rad/s, bobs ±0.08 m. Nothing on the floor under it **(Pete, 24 Nov: no circles under stars)**.
- Sound: "bing" pitched ×1.25, audible ~14 m.

## 4. Weapon pickups
- 8 pickups, all guns (`GUN_PICKS = 8`, `BAT_PICKS = 0`; the two bat pickups became guns when everyone got a bat, 26 Nov).
- Gun pool (random each time): MP5, Uzi, Vector, P90, UMP, Sting (SMGs), Dual Pistols and the Crossbow **(Pete, 27 Nov: the SMGs and crossbow from 1v1)**. A crossbow pickup holds 10 bolts; the rest hold 20 bullets.
- 20 bullets, no reloading. Empty → automatically back to the bat ("out of bullets: back to the bat").
- Walking over a gun takes it if you have no gun or yours has under 10 bullets. If yours still has 10+ and the one on the floor is a different gun, you can swap **(Pete, 26 Nov)**: the screen shows "E  Swap your MP5 (14) for the UMP (20)" (E, or X on a controller; rebindable as "Swap gun"); yours drops there with the bullets it had left and lies 20 s for anyone, like the dropped stars. Bots don't swap yet.
- Placement: 70% inside the house, 20% on the roof, 10% on the lawns, at least 10 m from other stars, pickups and players on the same floor **(Pete, 26 Nov: never grouped, mostly inside or on the roof; was 80% inside, 20% lawns, 6 m)**.
- Respawn: 15 s after being taken, somewhere new (`PICK_BACK = 15`).
- Look: weapon model floating, turning slowly, over a thin white ring (torus r 0.62 m) whose opacity pulses.
- Feed text: "You picked up a Vector (20 bullets)" / "picked up a bat".
- The bat is slot 1, a gun slot 2. Bots always hold a found gun when they have one.

### Gadgets **(Pete, 26 Nov)**
Six more pickups, placed and coming back like the guns: 2 proximity mines, 2 stun grenades, 2 speed boosts. You carry at most one mine and one stun grenade (you can't take another while you hold one); they're lost when you die. Numbers are the web game's lethals.
- **Proximity mine** (Q, or D-pad left; rebindable "Lay mine"): laid on the floor 1.1 m in front of you (at your feet if that's blocked), armed after 1 s (its red light blinks fast, then once every 2 s), goes off when anyone else comes within 1.7 m (1.1 m up or down): 60 damage at the middle down to 40% at the edge of 3.4 m, nothing through walls. A blast sets off other mines in reach. Fire, a flash and grey smoke.
- **Stun grenade** (G, or D-pad right; "Throw stun"): thrown along your aim, bursts 0.6 s after it lands (1.6 s at most): everyone within 6.5 m who can see it is stunned for 1 s (a quarter speed, can't attack, aim knocked out) and gets a white flash on screen; caught in your own, 0.5 s.
- **Speed boost**: taken at once, half as fast again for 10 s.
- **Right click uses your item too (Pete, 27 Nov)**: the aim button (right mouse, LT) lays your mine or throws your stun, whichever you picked up last (then the other); with neither, it aims as before. That press doesn't aim.
- The HUD's weapon card shows what you carry and the boost left ("Q / RMB  MINE   G  STUN   BOOST 7": the right-click one is marked). Bots throw a stun into a fight at 5–15 m and leave a mine behind as they move through the house.

## 5. Players
- 100 HP; regen after 3 s without being hit, 10 HP/s.
- Run 8.5 m/s **(Pete, 24 Nov: far too fast at the web's 12.7)**, ground acceleration 55, air 22; jump 12 m/s **(Pete)**; gravity 36; double jump. Players and bots share one speed. Weapon weight: SMGs +3–6%, Dual Pistols +8%.
- Downed: at 0 HP you go down instead of dying. Crawl slowly (1.2 m/s **(Pete, 27 Nov)**) with 30 downed HP; enemies can shoot you to death or finish you. Otherwise you get up on your own after 5 s **(Pete)** with the downed HP you had left. 15 s bleed-out timer exists only as a backstop. Downed players give off faint dark-red smoke, never green. A downed player must never visibly pass through walls.
- Finishers on a downed enemy (**not in the first build (Pete)**; a downed player is finished by shooting or hitting them): random, 25% each: Pancake, Heartbreaker, Roast, Combat. Can't be interrupted. "Eliminated" shows at the top as soon as one starts.
- Weapon skins from 1v1 **(Pete, 26 Nov)**: the bat is 1v1's barbed-wire bat (turned body, grip tape, a band and ring, two coils of barbed wire), and every bat (each spawn) and every gun (each time it's placed; it keeps its skin when it's dropped or swapped) gets one of six skins at random: Classic, Gold, Toxic, Candy, Galaxy, Lava. The Gold Bat hits twice as hard (100 a hit, so one hit downs) **(Pete, 26 Nov)**; gold has twinkling glitter and its name shows in gold on the HUD. A gun's coloured body takes the skin. The HUD and feed name the skin ("GOLD BAT", "a Lava UMP").
- Characters **(Pete, 24 Nov: the same cast as imok 1v1)**: Wednesday, Lil Pink, North West, Johnny Rotten, Emo Bunny, Glitch, Kitty, Puffa, Raven, Toxic — the 10 playable characters in 1v1's `CHARS` (main, commit `879f706`), each with its colour from there. Tripo models (same blocky style and six-part rig) are in the 1v1 repo's `chars/` and come in with the art pass, one at a time: each is joined into one body, rigged and moved with motion capture (§12, `characters/`). All ten are rigged **(Pete, 27 Nov)**. Wednesday is your character until character select exists (she's left out of the bots). Each match the 7 bots are drawn at random from the 10. The dev-only Shadow (roboid) isn't in the cast.

## 6. Shop / cash-in point
- Four shops, one in each corner of the front and back lawns, backed against the boundary hedge and facing the house: x ±56, z ±44.1 (house centred at 0,0; front = −z) **(Pete, 26 Nov)**. Only one is open at a time: it has the pulsing pad and a soft column of warm light over it, the minimap shows it (a gold-edged S, pinned to the rim when far off), and the HUD hint names it ("Cash in at the shop: front lawn, left"; left = −x).
- The open shop moves to one of the other three **(Pete, 26 Nov)**: every minute (never in the middle of a cash-in), when a cash-in there is cut short by a hit, and when stars dropped by a downed or killed player are picked up within 15 m of it. The kill feed says "The shop has moved: back left" (with the reason), with a soft gong.
- Same neon vending machine as the main game (~1.5× character height). Sells nothing; it's only the cash-in point.
- Cash in: hold 10 stars and stand within its use radius (~2–3 m). Win is instant.
- With 10 stars the HUD says "Cash in at the shop: <lawn>, <side>", and a centre message shows "10 stars / Get outside to the shop and cash them in" for 2.6 s.

## 7. Bots (8-player AI)
- Search like a person: a bot only knows about a star or weapon it has actually seen (line of sight, within 9 m Easy / 15 m Normal / 20 m Hard, under 4 m height difference, checked every 0.3 s). It remembers it until the item is taken.
- Priority each think:
  1. Holding 10 stars → run to the shop.
  2. Enemy in its face (under 7 m with fists/bat, under 13 m with a gun) → fight.
  3. Nearest known star or weapon. Distance = horizontal + 3 × vertical. Weapons count 4 m closer when unarmed. Ignore gun pickups while armed, and bats if it has one.
  4. Nothing known → explore room to room: random indoor spot 8–32 m away; re-pick after 10–16 s or on arrival.
- Keeps shooting at anyone further off while moving to its goal.
- Target choice: usual scoring (visible, recently shot by, downed, current target, revenge) plus a lean of 1.5 × stars (max 10) towards players with more stars. Marked players (8+) are always "seen" for tracking.
- Styles: melee/Rumble pool (rush, flank, etc.), not sniper/camper.
- Difficulty Easy/Normal/Hard. Easy: slower reactions, wider aim, no headshots, 30% less damage, no revenge hunts.
- Browser build bots spent ~30% of their time on the lawns. Keep them mostly indoors (bias goals inside), and let them use portals, the launch trampoline and lifts.

## 9. The Mansion
Full map: 1v1 repo README, "The Mansion". Key points:
- Plot 120 × 90 m; house ~80 × 54 m, red brick, two floors plus a walkable roof and roof terrace. Clipped-hedge grounds; fountain and lamp-lit drive out front.
- 15 rooms per floor on a 5 × 3 grid, each with its own colours, lighting and props, doorways in every wall so there are loops everywhere. Doorways are 4.4 m wide (web: 3.2) and furniture stands against the walls, clear of doors and windows, so every room is easy to run through **(Pete, 24 Nov)**.
- Ground floor: kitchen, dining room, foyer (front door, porch with 2 outer pillars), library, conservatory, games room, cinema, grand hall (double height, 4.4 m grand staircase), indoor pool (wadeable, springboard, no rubber ducks **(Pete, 25 Nov)**), gym (trampolines, launch trampoline up to the trophy room), bar (mostly smashable glass), ballroom (disco floor, moving lights, own music), gallery, music room, garage (black textured walls, flickering lights, 2 dirt bikes on wall racks).
- Upstairs: master bedroom, dressing room, landing, study, observatory (the space room: a ceiling of twinkling stars, and the telescope spins round when someone walks up to it, **Pete, 26 Nov**), playroom (ball pit), spa (hot tub), atrium walkway round the hall void, guest room, trophy room (the Hall of Fame: ten rock singers and guitarists as gig posters in glittery gold frames, **Pete, 26 Nov**), panic room, kids' room, hall of mirrors (mirrors on its walls; the web's zig-zag maze is gone, Pete 24 Nov), lab, roof terrace (the hot tub in the far corner away from the garage stairs and 25% bigger, no sun loungers, **Pete, 26 Nov**).
- Stairs are kept clear at both ends: nothing stands at the foot or the top of any staircase (the garage shelf, the kitchen drum and crate and the master bedroom wardrobe were taken out) **(Pete, 26 Nov)**.
- Vertical links: grand staircase, kitchen back stairs, garage stairs to the terrace, 2 ziplines from the top of the grand staircase up through the hall's skylights onto the roof (**Pete, 26 Nov**: jump on the yellow ring by its post to grab the handle; 11 m/s; you smash through a big skylight pane, which comes back 5 s later), 2 lifts, fireman's pole (both ways), launch trampoline, 2 portal pairs linking opposite corners, 3 outside ladders to the roof (2 front, 1 back) plus a terrace-to-roof ladder.
- Glass: no glass walls between rooms: they're open doorways now **(Pete, 24 Nov)**. Windows all round, 4.4 m wide on a 0.45 m sill up to 4.4 m (web: 3 m on 0.8 m), jump into one and it breaks, step over the sill once it's open **(Pete, 24 Nov)**; the side windows are fixed. 6 bulletproof glass floor panels upstairs, one-shot glass skylights in the roof.
- Lawns: only front and back are open; sides closed with invisible walls up past the roof edge. The cash-in shops stand in the four lawn corners against the boundary hedge, facing the house, with no lightbox on top **(Pete, 25 and 26 Nov)**.

Mansion rules **(Pete)**:
- Nothing covers windows or glass ceilings.
- Disco lights stay inside the ballroom.
- Stairs clear at top and bottom, with low guardrails.
- No black poles in doorways.
- Hot tubs: white stone surround, jets only, on a wooden deck, no ducks. You walk in and out without jumping **(Pete, 27 Nov)**: a low white stone step round the outside and a seat round the inside under the water, 0.28 m each, so it's deck → step → rim → seat → tub floor.
- Ball pit balls fly everywhere when you walk or jump in; wading through slowly, they roll out of your way **(Pete, 27 Nov)**.
- Loose things move **(Pete, 27 Nov)**: the 72 small things standing on a floor (stools, bins, barrels, floor lamps, small crates and side tables: up to 1.2 m across, 1.8 m tall and 1.4 m³) are physics objects. Run into one and it's shoved the way you're going (lighter ones go faster, up to 90% of your speed) and tall ones topple; shots, bat swings and mine blasts knock them about. They go home at each countdown, a broken one grows back at home, and anything knocked out of the grounds goes home.
- Must run smoothly with 8 players: only light the room you're in, cull rooms you can't see, batch static meshes.

## 10. Studio rules (apply to everything) (Pete)
- Keep the design as minimal and clean as possible (24 Nov): no decoration a rule doesn't need.
- No confetti, coloured particle squares or over-the-top sparks; hits use a red spray with a mist puff.
- No black zig-zag cracks or smoke on damaged buildings.
- All upright glass breaks with one hit and shatters into tiny shards that settle on surfaces; floor/roof glass is tougher.
- White ladders, not crate hop-ups.
- Realistic, premium look, never toy-like.
- Show Pete preview images before publishing anything new.
- End every reply with "Published" or "Unpublished".

## 12. Godot architecture
- **CollectionGame** (autoload): owns the config above, match state (countdown → fight → won), the count-up timer and the win check.
- **StarSpawner**: pool of 14 `Star` scenes (Area3D + mesh); candidate points baked from the NavigationMesh, filtered to the house volume.
- **PickupSpawner**: 6 gun + 2 bat `Pickup` scenes with respawn timers.
- **Fighter** script shared by Player and Bot: `stars: int`, `downed`, `alive`; signals `star_taken`, `downed`, `died`.
- **Bot AI** (`fighter/bot_brain.gd`): drives a Fighter through the same inputs you use; NavigationAgent3D on a navigation mesh baked at match start from the level's collision boxes, with a link up every ladder; sight memory by raycasts every 0.3 s; goal priority from section 7.
- **X-ray outline** for marked players: second material pass with depth test off (or stencil outline shader), shown only when stars ≥ 8.
- **Minimap**: top-down SubViewport or drawn 2D icons; gold star icons for marked players, clamped to the circle edge.

### Autoloads
| Name | Script | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Settings | `collection/autoload/settings.gd` | every saved option (view, graphics, display, sound, mouse and controller, crosshair and hits, bots, keys), saved to `user://settings.cfg`, with Reset all |

CollectionGame is **not** an autoload: it is a node (`collection/game/collection_game.gd`) that `main.gd` creates as "Game" and hands to every fighter. It runs the match: countdown (3 s) → fight → won, the count-up clock, fighters, stars, pickups, the shop cash-in, respawns, glass and the kill feed.
